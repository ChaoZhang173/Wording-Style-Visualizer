The rain began before the town had quite woken up. It tapped on the kitchen window, softly at first, as though it had forgotten whether anyone was home. Mira left her tea on the table and opened the back door.

Beyond the steps, the garden had changed its colors. The stones were almost black. A yellow leaf turned slowly in a shallow pool, making the same small journey again and again. Her neighbor was standing under the apple tree with an empty watering can.

Neither of them said anything. After a moment, he lifted the can in a modest salute. Mira laughed, and the morning, which had seemed so heavy a minute earlier, became an ordinary morning again.
